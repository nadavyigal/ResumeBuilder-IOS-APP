## App Review notes (private, replaces the current notes)

```
REVIEW PATH
1. Open Me > Sign In with the demo account above. It has already used its 3 free PDF downloads, so the purchase sheet appears on the first export.
2. On Home, import a text-based PDF resume and paste a job description. Accept the OpenAI consent request, then run the check.
3. Open the result, then Preview > Export PDF. The purchase sheet offers 5 PDF downloads, 14 PDF downloads, and Resumely Pro (monthly, auto-renewing, unlimited PDF downloads with a fair-use limit of 40 per month).
4. Buy any option in Apple Sandbox. After server verification, the PDF share sheet opens automatically.
5. Exporting the same tailored resume again is free. Me shows the plan, Restore Purchases, and Manage subscription for Pro.

Match Scores are free and unlimited. Only PDF downloads are paid.

In-app purchases: resumely.exports.5.v2 (consumable), resumely.exports.14 (consumable), resumely.pro.monthly (auto-renewable subscription). The purchase sheet links Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA) and our Privacy Policy. Public billing stays off until release; the demo account is enabled for this flow.

ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
Me > Sign In > Sign Up creates an email/password account. While signed in, Me > Account > Delete Account removes the account.

PRIVACY
The app requests consent before sending resume and job-description text to OpenAI.
```
